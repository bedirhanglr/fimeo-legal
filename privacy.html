Fimeo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Fimeo ("we," "us," "our") is a social app for people who love film and television. This policy explains what information we collect when you use Fimeo, why we collect it, and the choices you have — including how to delete your account and everything tied to it.

1Information We Collect

We collect only what Fimeo needs to work — there's no hidden data broker relationship, and we don't sell your information to anyone.

2Third-Party Services We Use

Fimeo is built on a small number of infrastructure providers. Each one processes a narrow slice of your data, strictly to provide their specific service to us:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and file storage (your account, content, and uploaded photos).
  • Sentry — receives crash reports and error logs so we can fix bugs. This can include device model, OS version, and the app state at the moment of a crash.
  • PostHog — receives anonymized-where-possible product analytics (which screens you visit, which features you use) so we can improve the app.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications using your device's push token.
  • Resend — sends transactional emails on our behalf (e.g. your sign-up confirmation code), using your email address.
  • RevenueCat — processes Fimeo Plus subscription purchases and manages entitlement status; it receives a device-generated purchaser ID and your purchase/transaction data from the App Store or Google Play, not your payment details (those are handled entirely by Apple/Google).
  • Apple / Google — if you choose "Sign in with Apple" or "Sign in with Google," they authenticate you and share your name/email with us as permitted by your own settings with them. They also process any Fimeo Plus purchase you make and handle your payment information directly — Fimeo never sees your card details.

We do not use any of the above for cross-app advertising tracking, and Fimeo does not show third-party ads.

3How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the core features of Fimeo — your feed, profile, library, and social interactions.
  • To keep the app secure and prevent abuse, spam, and fraudulent accounts.
  • To respond when you report content or another user, and to enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To send you notifications you've opted into (likes, comments, follows).
  • To understand, in aggregate, how people use Fimeo so we can improve it.

4Sharing Your Information

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers listed above (each bound to process it only for the purpose stated), and when required by law.

Content you choose to post publicly (posts, reviews, your public lists) is visible to other Fimeo users by design — that's the point of a social app. Your ratings and watched history are visible to you and, where a feature explicitly says so (e.g. Taste Match), to another user you've chosen to compare with.

5Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your profile, posts, comments, reviews, ratings, lists, and device tokens are permanently removed from our production database. Reports you've filed against other users/content are retained for a limited time for safety review purposes, disassociated from your account where possible.

6Your Rights

You can, at any time:

  • Edit your profile information directly in the app (Settings → Edit Profile).
  • Delete your account — Settings → Delete Account. This is permanent and immediate; there is no recovery period.
  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or ask us a question about this policy, by contacting us at support@fimeo.app.
If you're in the European Union or the United Kingdom, these rights are guaranteed to you under the GDPR. If you're in Türkiye, they're guaranteed under KVKK (Kişisel Verilerin Korunması Kanunu). We honor the same rights for every Fimeo user, everywhere, regardless of where you live.

7Children's Privacy

Fimeo is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your country to consent to data processing without parental permission). We don't knowingly collect information from children under that age. If you believe a child has created a Fimeo account, contact us and we'll remove it.

8Security

We protect your data with industry-standard measures — encrypted connections (TLS) for everything sent to and from the app, row-level database security so no user can read another user's private data, and access controls limiting who on our team can see raw data. No system is perfectly secure, but we take this seriously.

9Changes to This Policy

If we make a material change to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

10Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at support@fimeo.app.
