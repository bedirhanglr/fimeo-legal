Common questions

How do I report a post or a user?

Tap the ··· icon on any post, or the ··· icon on a profile, and choose Report. Pick a reason and add any details — we review every report.

How do I block someone?

From their profile or from any of their posts, tap ··· → Block. Their posts will stop appearing in your feed immediately, and they won't be notified.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete Account. This is permanent and removes your profile, posts, and everything you've added to Fimeo right away.

I forgot my password — what do I do?

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and follow the link we email you.
